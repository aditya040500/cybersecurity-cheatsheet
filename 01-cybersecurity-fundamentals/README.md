## Source and scope

Primary reference: [Google Cybersecurity Certificate playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLTZYG7bZ1u6ocTMdhDwwmfjaNv134KcWn). Its public feed exposed titles for the course modules and related videos, while transcripts and detailed lesson material were not available from the feed. Topic selection is cross-checked against Google's [published curriculum](https://grow.google/intl/en_in/cybersecurity-course), which lists risk management, networks, Linux and SQL, assets/threats/vulnerabilities, detection/response, and security automation. These notes summarize supported fundamentals and do not claim a video-by-video transcript.
